BukkiBooks customer health model

A simulated Customer Success case study for an accounting and invoicing SaaS business, focused on spotting adoption gaps before they become bigger customer risks.

THE APPROACH

  • Mapped alerts and check-ins to help the Customer Success team prioritise the right next action.

Dillali customer recovery workflows

A simulated wholesale ordering case study exploring how a Customer Success team can respond to declining activity and customer spend.

THE APPROACH

  • Included internal task assignment and support-ticket follow-up to create clear ownership.

MY STORY

From teaching chemistry to building better business systems.

My professional journey has taken me from the classroom into the world of CRM, workflow automation and customer operations. The connection between the two is something I value: making complex information easier to understand and creating structure that helps people move forward.

I enjoy looking at the full process — what happens when a lead comes in, how information is organised, where follow-up can break down and what a customer needs to succeed after signing up.

My work brings together CRM platforms, automation tools and Customer Success principles to help teams work with more clarity and consistency.

HOW I THINK ABOUT THE WORK

Technology is useful when it solves a real process problem.

I look beyond the automation itself. A good workflow needs a clear starting point, sensible conditions, an intended outcome and a plan for when a person needs to step in.
