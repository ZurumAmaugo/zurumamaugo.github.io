A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About Me

People, processes and the systems that connect them.

My professional journey began in education, where I worked as a Chemistry teacher. That experience shaped how I communicate, explain complex ideas and help people move through a learning process.

Today, I bring that same approach to CRM, workflow automation and Customer Success. I enjoy creating structure around customer information, improving follow-up processes and helping teams understand what needs attention.

I believe technology is most useful when it makes work easier for people. The goal is not automation for its own sake; it is clearer processes, better customer experiences and more confident teams.
